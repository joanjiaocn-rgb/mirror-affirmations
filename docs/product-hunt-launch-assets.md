## Product Hunt Copy

Tagline:

Private mirror affirmations for gentle self-talk.

Short description:

Mirror Affirmations is a no-recording browser demo for practicing kind self-talk with your own camera preview.

Maker comment draft:

Hi Product Hunt,

I built Mirror Affirmations as a small privacy-first experiment around self-talk. Most affirmation tools feel like quote apps. I wanted to test whether the practice feels more personal when you can see your own face, choose a short prompt, and say it out loud in a quiet browser preview.

The web demo does not record, save, or upload video. There is no account and no social feed. It is intentionally small: choose a prompt, adjust the text, practice once, and stop when it feels complete.

I am using the waitlist to learn whether people want a future local-only mobile app with private video history and reminders.

I would love feedback on:

- Whether the mirror practice feels clear from the landing page.
- Whether the privacy message feels trustworthy enough.
- Which prompt categories feel most useful.

Thanks for taking a look.
