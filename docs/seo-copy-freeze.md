## 1. Copy Strategy

Mirror Affirmations should sound private, quiet, and practical. The copy should not feel like a generic wellness SaaS page. It should quickly answer:

- What is it? A private mirror affirmation practice.
- Who is it for? People who want gentle self-talk with their own face and voice.
- Why is it safe to try? The web demo does not record, save, or upload video.
- What should the visitor do? Start the private practice or join the waitlist.

## 2. Forbidden Claims

Do not use:

- cure anxiety
- treat depression
- heal trauma
- diagnose your mood
- detect your emotions
- read your face
- therapy replacement
- clinically proven
- guaranteed confidence

Preferred safe language:

- private self-care practice
- gentle self-talk
- mirror practice
- emotional check-in
- reflection
- calming routine
- not a medical or therapy service

## 10. Design Handoff Notes

- Keep the homepage copy visible and calm; avoid oversized hype sections.
- The demo entry should be visible in the first viewport.
- The privacy line must appear before the camera permission action.
- SEO pages should not hide all useful content behind tabs.
- FAQ content should be crawlable HTML.
- CTA language should stay concrete: "Start private practice", "Practice without camera", "Join waitlist".
