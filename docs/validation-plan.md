# Validation Plan - Mirror Affirmations

Date: 2026-07-30
Stage: Pre-app validation

## 1. Validation Question

Will users open a private camera mirror, read a gentle prompt to themselves, and want to come back or join a waitlist?

## 2. What To Build First

Build only:

- SEO website.
- No-recording web demo.
- Waitlist form.
- Analytics.
- Privacy page.

Do not build:

- Native app.
- Recording.
- Cloud storage.
- Account login.
- Subscription.
- AI summary.

## 3. First 14 Days

### Day 0-2: Internal Test

- Test on iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, desktop Chrome.
- Confirm camera permission flow.
- Confirm camera stops after leaving demo.
- Ask 3-5 target users to read 5 prompts out loud.
- Remove prompts that feel awkward, fake, or too motivational.

### Day 3-5: Private Seed Test

Invite 20-30 people from small trusted groups.

Ask them to do one task:

"Open the demo, turn on the camera if you feel comfortable, choose a prompt, and read it out loud once."

Collect:

- Did they allow camera?
- Did they finish a practice?
- Which prompt did they choose?
- Did they feel calm, awkward, moved, or nothing?
- Would they want reminders or local recording?

### Day 6-10: Search and Partner Landing

Publish SEO pages and submit sitemap.

Start low-risk outreach:

- Journaling newsletters.
- Self-care blogs.
- Meditation teachers.
- Yoga teachers.
- Wellness resource pages.

Message angle:

"I built a free private mirror affirmation practice. It does not record or upload video. It may be useful as a simple self-care exercise for your readers."

### Day 11-14: Review

Review:

- Demo starts.
- Camera permission grant rate.
- Prompt category clicks.
- Waitlist conversion.
- Qualitative reactions.
- Any privacy concerns.

## 4. Decision Thresholds

### Build iOS App If

- At least 300 qualified visits.
- At least 40 percent click demo start.
- At least 50 percent of demo starters grant camera permission.
- At least 8 percent join waitlist.
- At least 5 users ask for local recording, reminders, or history.

### Iterate If

- Visitors read SEO pages but do not start demo.
- Camera permission rate is weak but no-camera practice gets engagement.
- Users like the prompts but dislike seeing their face.

Possible iterations:

- Add no-camera practice as primary option.
- Soften camera CTA language.
- Improve privacy explanation before permission.
- Make bedtime use case the main positioning.

### Stop Or Reposition If

- Camera permission rate stays very low.
- Users say the concept feels cringe or performative.
- Waitlist conversion is below 3 percent after 300 qualified visits.
- Most interest is only in reading prompts, not mirror practice.
